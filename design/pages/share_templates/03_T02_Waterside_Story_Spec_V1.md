# T02 · 水边故事 · Spec V1

Status: **FROZEN — CONTENT / BEHAVIOR**
Visual status: **PARTIAL — recovered iterations, no unique final binary**

## 1. Role

T02 turns a selected fishing period into a photo-led memory story.

The photos are the primary content.

Statistics support the story and must not overpower it.

## 2. Photos

Use:
- 1–3 real user fishing photos from the selected range.

Priority:
- real catch environment;
- person + fish;
- fishing companions;
- water/shore context;
- authentic memory moments.

Do not replace the real-photo story with Fish Sticker ranking.

## 3. Statistics

Prior frozen requirement:

Show only light summary metrics:
- total catch count;
- total species count;
- largest catch.

The three metrics must not become a rigid dashboard-like three-column block when that harms photo hierarchy.

Later V2 direction:
- distribute/lighten stats;
- keep photos dominant;
- lower the visual weight of “largest catch”.

## 4. Species coverage

All species in the selected range must be represented with counts.

Preferred semantic form:
`鱼种 × 数量`

Do not omit minor species.

## 5. Time / place

Include:
- selected time range;
- location when supported by the underlying records.

Do not invent a location from visual background.

## 6. Story copy

Personal story copy must come from the user.

Do not generate a false first-person memory such as:
- “第一次夜钓…”
- “和朋友一起…”
- “那条刷新纪录的草鱼…”

unless the user actually provided/selected that content.

Fixed neutral template copy is allowed only when it does not pretend to be the user’s memory.

## 7. Media treatment

Photo panels may represent real image/video content only.

Do not display a play icon or duration badge unless the source item is actually video.

Do not add handwritten photo captions unless they are user-provided or explicitly selected.

## 8. Visual boundary

Prior direction:
- 9:16 share artifact;
- real photos dominate;
- natural lake/mountain atmosphere;
- blue-gray + restrained warm-gold;
- editorial/photo design rather than UI dashboard.

Recovered visual iterations include both light and heavy-glass experiments. They remain references until explicit visual selection/freeze.
