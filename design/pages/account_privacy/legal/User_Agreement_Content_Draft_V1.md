# 用户协议 · Content Draft V1

Status: **LEGAL_REVIEW_REQUIRED**
Design structure: **FROZEN**
Production legal copy: **NOT APPROVED**

This file defines the required agreement architecture and product-specific clauses to be reviewed and completed by legal counsel. It does not claim to be final production legal text.

## 1. Document metadata

Required before production approval:
- agreement version;
- effective date;
- contracting/operator entity;
- contact / dispute-notice channel;
- jurisdiction / governing-law language approved by counsel.

## 2. Acceptance and scope

Explain:
- agreement applies to use of YuJian;
- user should read and understand it before using account/service functions;
- Privacy Policy is a separate but related document.

Do not treat merely viewing the agreement page as consent.

## 3. Service description

Current product capabilities may include:
- photo-based fish recognition;
- FishRecord creation and management;
- Fish Guide / fish knowledge;
- profile/account settings;
- optional location attachment;
- optional AI model-improvement participation.

Service availability may change by version.

## 4. Account registration and security

Cover:
- accurate registration information;
- safeguarding credentials;
- responsibility for authorized account use;
- reporting suspected unauthorized access;
- password-change capability where available.

Do not expose internal account IDs or security implementation details.

## 5. User content

Cover user responsibility for content they choose to submit, including:
- catch photos;
- species corrections;
- notes and other fish-record information.

The agreement must require lawful use without making unsupported ownership/licensing claims.

Any license needed for service operation or optional model-improvement use must be reviewed carefully and must align with explicit consent/privacy policy.

## 6. AI recognition limitations

Required product-specific disclosure:

Fish recognition is an AI-assisted result and may be incorrect.

The agreement should make clear that users should not rely solely on recognition results for high-stakes decisions, including where relevant:
- food safety;
- toxicity/edibility;
- protected-species or legal compliance;
- emergency/survival decisions.

YuJian's recognition output is not a substitute for qualified local expert or official guidance where accuracy is safety- or law-critical.

Exact liability language requires legal review.

## 7. Fish knowledge content

Fish Guide / knowledge content is informational.

Legal review should define appropriate limitations for:
- biological information;
- fishing guidance;
- local rules that can change over time.

The product should not imply that static knowledge content supersedes current local fishing laws/regulations.

## 8. AI model improvement

Current product rule:
- optional;
- default OFF unless explicit valid consent exists;
- user may withdraw;
- refusal/withdrawal does not block recognition, FishRecord save or correction.

Agreement language must remain aligned with Privacy Policy and consent UI.

## 9. Location

Current product rule:
- requested only after explicit user action such as `使用当前位置`;
- used for the current FishRecord location purpose;
- denial does not block recognition or saving FishRecord.

No background tracking term is authorized by current design.

## 10. Prohibited use

Legal review should cover, as applicable:
- unlawful use;
- infringement of others' rights;
- malicious interference/security abuse;
- fraudulent/false account use;
- content that violates applicable law.

Do not add overly broad restrictions unrelated to the product without legal review.

## 11. Intellectual property

Legal review must distinguish:
- YuJian software/brand/service IP;
- user-owned content;
- licenses strictly needed to provide the service;
- any optional model-improvement license/permission aligned with explicit consent.

## 12. Service change / suspension / termination

Cover:
- product updates/changes;
- availability interruptions;
- account restriction/termination where legally/product appropriate.

Do not promise permanent availability.

## 13. Logout vs account deletion

Logout is not account deletion.

Delete Account is currently a **DEFERRED** in-product flow.

Final agreement must describe account termination/deletion only in ways supported by actual operations and legal requirements.

## 14. Minors

Requires legal review for launch jurisdictions.

No age threshold is frozen by this design file.

## 15. Privacy

Cross-reference the approved Privacy Policy.

Agreement and Privacy Policy must not conflict about:
- AI model improvement;
- location;
- user content;
- data sharing/retention.

## 16. Disclaimers / liability

Counsel must draft final clauses.

Design/Product only freezes the need to address:
- AI recognition uncertainty;
- informational Fish Guide content;
- network/service availability;
- legally required non-waivable rights.

## 17. Agreement changes

Define versioning/effective-date/update notice rules after legal review.

## 18. Contact / dispute / governing law

All operator, dispute-resolution and governing-law details require counsel approval.
