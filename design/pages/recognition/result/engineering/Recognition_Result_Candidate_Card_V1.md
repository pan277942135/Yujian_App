# Recognition Result Candidate Card V1.1

Status: **FROZEN**

Machine-readable authority: `candidate_card_contract.json`.

Candidate cards are Medium-only and remain inside one unified Candidate Glass
Panel with the prompt and the other-species action.

## States

- Default: calm Glass card;
- Suggested: first model candidate may be visually suggested but is not selected;
- Selected: explicit user selection, restrained Morning Gold treatment;
- Pressed / Disabled / Focus: additive shared interaction states.

There is no confidence percentage, rarity treatment, carousel, or automatic
selection. Tapping a candidate commits the selection and exposes Metadata,
Story, and the Dual CTA. The Species Selector is a separate authority: its
selected state remains Teal `#0F7A78` and must not inherit Medium Gold.
